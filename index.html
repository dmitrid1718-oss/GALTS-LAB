Does John Galt survive AI?

Ten imagined remarks from Ayn Rand, written as provocations for our era. Ten questions about reason, ownership, labor, freedom, and what makes a human life valuable.

Imagined, not quoted. Ayn Rand died in 1982. The following remarks are original thought experiments inspired by themes in her philosophy—not statements she actually made. We invite disagreement, corrections, and stronger arguments.
01

The Thinking Machine

“You have created a machine that can imitate reason. Now tell me: does it understand reality, or merely reproduce the thoughts of those who do?”

THE CHALLENGE Does intelligence require independent understanding?
